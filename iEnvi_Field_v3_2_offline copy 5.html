Fast start
Tap Start now, then Use my GPS, pick a Category, and Save. Add photos if helpful. Export to CSV/JSON or Google Sheets when online.

Quick Help

1) Location

Tap Use my GPS for a quick fix, then drag the marker on the map to refine. For better accuracy, tap Improve Accuracy (10s) and keep the phone still with clear sky.

2) Photos

Add up to 5 photos per record. Tap a thumbnail to rename its caption. Photos are resized for speed and stored with the record.

3) Offline Maps

KML/KMZ adds data points/lines/polygons, and KMZ can include image overlays. To carry a basemap offline, use KMZ Ground Overlay (from Google Earth) or an MBTiles file exported from your GIS tool. Then tap Upload Map.

4) Save & Export

Records save instantly to your device (IndexedDB). Export CSV/JSON locally or, when online and signed in, export to Drive/Sheets.

Tips for accuracy

  • Enable device “High accuracy” / “Precise Location”.
  • Step outdoors, avoid metal roofs and vehicles when possible.
  • Use Improve Accuracy (10s) to take multiple readings and keep the best.
  • Take a photo: if EXIF GPS is present, we can use it to snap the point.

Offline Maps & Overlays

Upload one of the following:

  • KML / KMZ (points/lines/polygons). KMZ may include a GroundOverlay (image basemap).
  • GeoJSON (vector features).
Note: This does not download Nearmap/Google imagery. For imagery, export a KMZ Ground Overlay from Google Earth (it embeds an image), or consult your licensed imagery provider to export an MBTiles / tile package your team is allowed to carry offline.

    Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) 
    
==============================
iEnvi Field Sample — Quick SOP
==============================

1) Before you start
- Confirm job name, site address, and client folder in Google Drive.
- Check your phone battery (>50%) and storage space.
- PPE: As required by site induction (hard hat, steel-caps, hi‑vis, gloves, eye protection).
- Instruments: PID, water level meter, EC/pH meter, thermometer, turbidity tube if needed.
- Sampling gear: liners, scoops, tubing, decon solution, wipes, bags, labels, esky & ice packs.

2) Getting your first point
- Tap “Work Offline” if not signed in; you can sync later.
- Tap “Use my GPS” (allow permission). Wait for fix.
- Tap “Improve Accuracy (10s)” and keep still outdoors if accuracy > 15 m.
- Drag the map pin to refine location if needed (when the map is available).

3) Choose the right category
- Site Feature: anything not a sample, like a tank pit, building corner, spill area.
- Soil: e.g., Borehole (BH), Test Pit (TP), Surface Sample (SS). Use soil profile section.
- Water: e.g., Monitoring Well (MW), Surface Water (SW). Fill the water parameter block.

4) Sample IDs
- Soil/Water categories use IDs like BH01, TP12, MW03, etc. Prefix auto-fills from the type.
- Number auto suggests the next available (01–99). Change if needed.
- For soil layers, tick “Sample collected” then add Primary ID; add QS for field duplicates.

5) Photos
- Add up to 5 per record. Capture the sample point, the surrounding context, and label jar/esky.
- Tap a photo to rename its caption, e.g., “BH02 at 1.5 m — sample jar”.
- Try to include the well ID or borehole name in one photo if visible.

6) Notes (plain English)
- Use the microphone if typing is slow.
- Soil: “SAND with silt, moist, brown. No odour. PID 0.0 ppm.”
- Water: “MW03—clear, no odour, pH 7.2, EC 1200 µS/cm, water level 2.45 mbgs.”

7) Save
- Tap “Save record” — it is stored on your phone (device-only) until you export/sync.
- You can export later to CSV/JSON or, when signed in online, to Google Sheets with photos.

8) Export to Google Sheets (online only)
- Sign in, pick the Drive folder (Shared Drive OK).
- Tap “Export data & photos to Google Sheets” — opens your new sheet with photo links.

9) Offline basemaps
- KML adds vector features only (points/lines/polygons).
- KMZ (from Google Earth) can include an image with a Lat/Lon box (GroundOverlay)—we place it.
- For large offline imagery, use MBTiles exported from licensed sources (respect licensing).

10) Improving GPS accuracy (what actually helps)
- Enable device “High accuracy” or “Precise Location” in settings.
- Stand away from vehicles/buildings; avoid under metal roofs.
- Hold phone steady and flat; avoid body shielding the sky.
- Give the phone 10–20 seconds outdoors to acquire satellites.
- Our app samples multiple fixes and picks the best; it shows “±X m” when available.

Definitions
- mbgs: metres below ground surface.
- LNAPL: Light Non‑Aqueous Phase Liquid (e.g., fuel) thickness in the well.
- PID: Photo‑ionisation detector reading in ppm; indicates volatile organics.

Common pitfalls
- Forgetting to set the location before saving → Save is disabled until a location & type are set.
- Photos too large → we automatically resize for speed.
- Testing from a local file: the map library might not load offline; use “Use my GPS” button anyway.
- Expecting KML to include aerial imagery → it does not, unless it’s a KMZ GroundOverlay.

Data retention & privacy
- Records are stored locally in your browser’s storage (IndexedDB).
- Deleting browser data or using a different device will lose unsynced records.
- Always export to CSV/JSON or Sheets at the end of day if data matters.
